# 🗄️ Database Engineering & Data Modeling

> **Status:** Category Index (Evergreen)  
> **Deskripsi:** Prinsip desain skema database, pengoptimalan query SQL, sistem caching, dan penyimpanan NoSQL.

## 🗺️ Sub-Domain Pengetahuan
1. **Relational Databases (PostgreSQL / MySQL)**: Normalisasi skema, Indexing (B-Tree, GIN, GiST), & EXPLAIN ANALYZE.
2. **In-Memory & Key-Value Caching (Redis / Memcached)**: Caching Strategies (Cache-Aside, Write-Through), TTL, & Pub/Sub.
3. **NoSQL Systems (MongoDB / Cassandra)**: Document Stores, Wide-Column Stores, & Event Sourcing.
4. **Data Replication & Partitioning**: Master-Replica, Connection Pooling, & Horizontal Sharding.
