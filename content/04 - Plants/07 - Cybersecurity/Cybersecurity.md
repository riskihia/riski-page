# 🔒 Cybersecurity & Application Security

> **Status:** Category Index (Evergreen)  
> **Deskripsi:** Prinsip keamanan aplikasi web, autentikasi, enkripsi, dan mitigasi kerentanan perangkat lunak.

## 🗺️ Sub-Domain Pengetahuan
1. **OWASP Top 10 Mitigation**: SQL Injection, XSS, CSRF, Broken Access Control, & SSRF.
2. **Authentication & Authorization**: OAuth2, OpenID Connect, JWT, Session Management, & RBAC.
3. **Cryptography Basics**: Hashing (Argon2, bcrypt), Asymmetric vs Symmetric Encryption, TLS/SSL.
4. **Security Hardening**: Rate Limiting, CORS Policy, Content Security Policy (CSP), & Secret Management.
